## Summary

Enable first-time visitors to understand the site's purpose and see available volunteer roles through clear home page copy and a dedicated roles discovery page.

## Expected Outcome

First-time visitors can understand what the site is for and identify which volunteer roles match their interests. **Baseline: unknown.** The coordinator will ask 5 first-time visitors during the first pilot weekend whether they can articulate what the site is for. **Target:** to be agreed after pilot data. **Owner:** Coordinator. **Evaluation window:** Post-pilot (separate from delivery acceptance).

## Scope

R1: Home page displays clear, concise copy explaining the organization's purpose and volunteer opportunity.
R2: Roles discovery page lists four volunteer roles (driver, kitchen, companion, admin) with one-line descriptions each.
R3: No sign-up form yet (future work).
R4: Partner-provided content and email reminders remain as future discovery (partner access not confirmed).

## Proposed Design / Approach

**Stage 1 (designed):** Create and deploy home page copy that clearly explains what the site is for. **Stage 2 (designed):** Build and deploy roles page showing the four volunteer opportunities with descriptions. **Stage 3 (stub):** Later discover and scope partner-provided content and email reminder system.

## Verification Plan

- Home page copy is deployed and explains the site's purpose
- Roles page is deployed and lists all four roles with one-line descriptions
- Coordinator completes first pilot survey of 5 first-time visitors
