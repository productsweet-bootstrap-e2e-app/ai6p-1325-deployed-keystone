## Summary

Write and integrate clear, accessible copy on the home page that explains what the site is for to a first-time visitor.

## Problem Or Opportunity

First-time visitors land on the home page without a clear understanding of the site's purpose or mission.

## Scope

R1: Write purpose statement explaining the site's core mission in plain, welcoming language. R2: Place above the fold on the home page. R3: Ensure accessible contrast and readability. R4: Include a clear link or call-to-action pointing to the volunteer roles page.

## Proposed Design / Approach

1. Draft 2–3 purpose statement options for owner review. 2. Finalize based on feedback. 3. Integrate into home-page template at the top section. 4. Test readability and accessibility (contrast, screen-reader verification).

## Verification Plan

1. Purpose statement is visible above the fold on home page (screenshot or live verification). 2. Text explains what the site is for in plain language (owner or UX review). 3. Text meets WCAG AA contrast standards. 4. Screen reader correctly reads the purpose statement. 5. Link to roles page is present and functional.
