## Summary

Add a one-line description under the welcome heading on the keystone home page that tells first-time visitors what the site does, and link it to the getting-started guide. Today new visitors see only the heading and leave without understanding what the site offers.

## Scope

Home page welcome section only: one sentence of copy and one link. No layout or navigation changes.

## Problem Or Opportunity

New visitors to the keystone home page encounter only a welcome heading with no explanation of what the site does. This lack of context causes them to leave without understanding the site's value or purpose. First-time users are most affected, as they have no prior knowledge of keystone's offerings and rely on the home page to orient themselves.

Currently, there is no pathway for these visitors to discover what the site does or how to get started. Adding a brief descriptive line and a link to the getting-started guide would address this gap by immediately communicating the site's purpose and providing a clear next step for exploration.
