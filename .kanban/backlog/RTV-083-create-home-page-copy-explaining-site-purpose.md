## Summary

Update home page to clearly explain what the site is for and why people should volunteer

## Problem Or Opportunity

First-time visitors arriving at the home page don't understand what the site is for or why they should volunteer.

## Scope

R1: Home page displays clear, concise copy explaining the organization's purpose and volunteer opportunity.
R2: Copy is positioned prominently above the fold.
R3: No sign-up form on the home page (roles discovery page comes next).

## Proposed Design / Approach

Write 1–2 sentences of clear, jargon-free copy explaining what the site does and why people should volunteer. Place it prominently on the home page before any other content.

## Verification Plan

- Home page displays updated purpose copy
- Copy is visible above the fold
- Copy clearly articulates the purpose in plain language
